Carnegie Mellon University / Current research

NOMORA.

Nearshore Operations for Multimodal Autonomous Robotic Agents

In development · 2026-present

At CMU, I work with Prof. Matt Travers on exploration and field robotics. NOMORA studies hybrid ground-air robots for unknown coastal environments, with an autonomous surface vessel as a supporting platform.

M.S. Robotics · Robotics Institute

Coastal exploration
across land, air, and water.

I’m interested in how different forms of mobility can help robots explore places that are difficult to reach. NOMORA brings that interest into my graduate research.
