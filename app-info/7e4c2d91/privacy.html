Personal AI Office handles Google user data only for its owner’s authorized productivity requests.

Access and use

Granted permissions may cover Gmail messages, attachments, labels and drafts; Calendar events; Drive files and metadata; and Tasks lists and tasks. Data is used to search, summarize, organize and manage this information as requested.

Processing and sharing

Task-relevant information may be processed by Google and the owner’s configured AI and private-delivery providers to perform those requests. Data is not sold or used for advertising or training generalized AI models. Use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Credentials and retained records are protected by encryption and access controls as appropriate. Working data, results and backups remain in private storage until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked in Google Account connections; revocation does not delete retained copies, which must be removed separately.

These pages

There are no tracking scripts, analytics or forms. The hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata. Material changes to data use require an updated policy and any required consent.
